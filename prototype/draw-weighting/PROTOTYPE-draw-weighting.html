How should a Run pick its 5 Game states?

Each Round of the Ranked trainer draws one Eligible Game state from the SALET tree. These are the 283 states that aren't the opener and have at least 3 Candidates. This page lets you change how strongly common states are favoured, which turn each Round draws from, and what may repeat. Then draw Runs, or 1,000 of them, and judge how they feel.

The weight of a state is Candidatesα. With α = 1 this is Path frequency: the same as picking a random Answer and walking its Path. With α = 0 every state is equally likely.
